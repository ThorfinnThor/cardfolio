# Backlink plan (2026-10-22)

Links from other sites are the biggest ranking lever for a new site, and the one that cannot be built into the code. Nothing here is automated or posted for you: every post is yours to publish, under your name, following each community's rules. Do not buy links and do not post the same text in several places; both can get the site demoted.

## What to link to

| Goal | Page |
|---|---|
| Gift buyers | `https://yourcardbinder.com/` and the gift guides (`/ratgeber/pokemon-karten-verschenken/`, `/ratgeber/personalisiertes-pokemon-geschenk/`) |
| Collectors | `/ratgeber/pokemon-sammlung-starten/`, `/ratgeber/cardmarket-oder-cardtrader/` |
| Parents | `/ratgeber/pokemon-karten-fuer-kinder/` |
| English readers | `https://yourcardbinder.com/en/` and the guides under `/en/guides/` |

## Channels, in the order I would try them

1. **Your own properties (free, fast).** Link the site from the GitHub repository (website field and README), from your other sites if they fit (stargazingindex.com, besttravelclimate.com, a short "other projects" line), and from your email signature. These links are weak alone but show crawlers the site is connected.
2. **Tool directories.** Submit the tool (not the guides) to general directories of free web tools and to AlternativeTo-style comparison sites. Describe what it does in one sentence, say it is free and needs no account.
3. **Communities for Pokémon card collectors (Reddit, Discord, forums).** Read the rules first: many forbid self-promotion. Useful pattern: answer real questions ("how many pockets do I need?", "Cardmarket or CardTrader?") with a helpful answer and, only where allowed, a link to the matching guide. Say that you built the site; the CardTrader links carry an affiliate code, so disclose that where you share them.
4. **German-speaking audiences.** Parents' and gift-idea forums, Pokémon fan blogs and YouTube channels about card collecting. Offer something concrete: the personalised-gift guide as a resource, or a short guest section such as "How to plan a Pokémon birthday gift".
5. **Digital PR with a hook.** A small data piece from the site's own data, for example "which Pokémon have the most cards" (the Pokémon pages already count cards and years), pitched to card-collecting blogs and newsletters.
6. **Product listings.** A launch post on a maker community (Product Hunt-style) once you are happy with the tool; one shot, so prepare screenshots first.

## Draft texts (edit before using)

**Short description (directories)**
> Your Card Binder: pick a favourite Pokémon (or up to four), see the cards as a binder page and get the shopping list prepared for Cardmarket, CardTrader or TCGplayer. Free, no account, binders stay in your browser. Independent project, not affiliated with The Pokémon Company.

**Answer in a community thread (only where links are allowed)**
> A 9-pocket page is the usual standard, and a sheet has two sides, so 20 sheets hold 360 cards. I wrote a short explanation with a table here: https://yourcardbinder.com/en/guides/binder-pocket-sizes/ (I run that site; it is free and has no ads).

**Pitch to a blog (German)**
> Hallo, ich betreibe yourcardbinder.com, ein kostenloses Werkzeug, mit dem man einen Pokémon-Binder als Geschenk zusammenstellt und die Kaufliste für Cardmarket oder CardTrader vorbereitet. Für Ihre Leser könnte der Ratgeber „Personalisiertes Pokémon-Geschenk“ interessant sein, weil er erklärt, warum bedruckte Karten keine gute Idee sind und wie man stattdessen mit der Auswahl persönlich wird. Ich beantworte gern Rückfragen oder schreibe einen Gastbeitrag.

## Measuring

- Search Console → Links shows which sites link to you and which pages they point at.
- Analytics → Acquisition shows referral traffic (only visitors who agreed to measuring).
- Check once a month; links from small, relevant sites matter more than one big unrelated one.

## Limits and risks

- Reddit and forum posts are `nofollow` links: they bring visitors and brand searches, not direct ranking power.
- Mention the CardTrader affiliate code wherever you share CardTrader links, as the programme and consumer law expect.
- Pokémon names and card images belong to their owners; the site states that it is independent. Do not use card images in outreach material.
